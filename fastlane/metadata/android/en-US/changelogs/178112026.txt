A quieter, safer beta: security hardening under the hood and a lighter widget on your battery.

Changed:
- "Add leg" buttons on trip screens now say "Add leg or charge" in every language
- The widget re-renders its background only when the car's look or charging state changes — less memory and battery use

Security:
- Server credentials are no longer included in Android backups or device transfers
- Debug-only components and logs can no longer expose your API token
